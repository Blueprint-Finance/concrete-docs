The vaults listed in the Live Vaults section of the Earn app are active vaults available through the Concrete platform. These vaults provide access to on-chain strategies, asset type, or market opportunity.

Users can review available Live Vaults, compare relevant vault attributes of [APY](/glossary/#apy), [TVL](/glossary/#tvl), withdrawal queue (if any) and exposure.
