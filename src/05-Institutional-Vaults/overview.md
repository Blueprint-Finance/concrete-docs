**Concrete Institutional Vaults** are vaults displayed on the Concrete platform that are designed for specific institutional, professional, or otherwise permissioned participants, and may include strategies or asset types that require additional access controls, eligibility checks, or approval before a user can participate. These permissions depend on the vault.

In the Earn application, Institutional Vaults appear as **"Permission Required"** to indicate that access is restricted. Users who do not have the required permissions will not be able to deposit into these vaults. Examples of Institutional Vaults may include permissioned RWA-focused vaults, such as **[RWA](/glossary/#rwa) [USD1](/glossary/#usd1)**, where access is limited to approved participants. If you wish to participate in such an offering, please contact [support@blueprintfinance.com](mailto:support@blueprintfinance.com) for more information on our eligibility criteria.
