Connect your wallet, choose a vault, and deposit supported assets to start earning automated, risk-adjusted yield with a single deposit.

After you deposit, the vault puts your assets to work in the yield strategy — no further action required. The steps below walk you through the full process, from opening the app to receiving your vault share tokens.

## Supported assets

Each vault accepts a specific asset, for example **WBTC Vault** accepts WBTC, and the **Concrete DeFi USDT** vault accepts USDT. Some vaults have Enso integration, meaning you can bridge, and/or swap and deposit into the vault using all of the listed tokens. See [Bridge and deposit with Enso](https://docs.concrete.xyz/Vaults/bridging-with-enso/) for more.
 

## Before you start
- You need a Web3 wallet (e.g. MetaMask) connected to Concrete. 
- Wallet connection on Earn requires Terms of Use and Privacy Policy acceptance, and the address is screened via Chainalysis for high-risk or restricted-jurisdiction addresses.
- Ensure you have sufficient tokens for any network fees, for example ETH for Ethereum network deposit fees. 

## Deposit steps
1. **Go to the Earn page** Open app.concrete.xyz/earn (https://app.concrete.xyz/earn) in your browser.
2. **Connect your wallet** Click Connect Wallet and approve the connection request in your wallet.
3. **Select a vault** Review the available vaults. Choose the one that matches the asset you want to deposit.
4. **Enter the amount you wish to deposit**
5. **Approve the token** If this is your first deposit with this token, you will need to submit an approval transaction allowing the vault contract to spend your tokens. Confirm this in your wallet.
6. **Confirm the deposit** Review the deposit summary and click Deposit. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.
7. **Receive ct[Asset] tokens** Once the transaction confirms, you receive ct[Asset] tokens (for example, ctDefiUSDT or ctWBTC) in your wallet. These tokens represent your proportional share of the vault and appreciate in value as the vault earns yield. 

## What happens after you deposit
Once deposited, your capital is put to work across the yield strategies listed on the vault page. The number of ct[Asset] tokens you hold doesn't change — instead, each token becomes worth more as the vault generates returns.
