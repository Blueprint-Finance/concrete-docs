# Concrete Points Program

The Concrete Points Program rewards participation across the Concrete ecosystem through on-chain activity and community engagement. This page explains how you earn Concrete Points through eligible protocol activity and Bags through social quests.

## Bags and Concrete Points

### Concrete Points

You earn Concrete Points through eligible on-chain activity in the Concrete protocol, including deposits into eligible Concrete vaults.

### Bags

You earn Bags by completing eligible social quests and community actions in the campaign powered by [Fuul](https://fuul.xyz).

Bags and Concrete Points are tracked separately during the campaign. Bags will be converted into Concrete Points later in the campaign.

## Social Campaign

To earn Bags, connect your wallet and the required social accounts through the Concrete Points campaign powered by Fuul.

### 1. Connect your wallet and accounts

Connect the wallet you want to associate with your campaign activity. Link any social accounts required for the quests you want to complete. Keep your accounts connected so eligible activity can be tracked.

### 2. Complete social actions

Bags reward eligible social and community actions. Examples may include:

- **Follow Concrete on X** – follow [Concrete on X](https://x.com/ConcreteXYZ) for updates.
- **Join the Concrete Discord** – join the [Concrete Discord](https://discord.gg/concretexyz) to discuss the protocol and share feedback.
- **Write a blog article about Concrete** – use the rubric provided in Discord to participate in the Article of the Week campaign.

Some quests have prerequisites. If a task is marked **Locked**, complete the required action first to unlock it.

## Earning Concrete Points On-Chain

The on-chain program is active alongside the social campaign. You earn Concrete Points directly through eligible participation in the Concrete protocol, including deposits into eligible vaults through the [Concrete app](https://app.concrete.xyz).

These Concrete Points are separate from the Bags you earn through social quests. See [Deposit into Vaults](./deposit.md) for deposit instructions.
