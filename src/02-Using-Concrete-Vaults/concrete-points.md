# Concrete Points Program

Welcome to the **Concrete Points Program!**

The Concrete Points Program rewards users for participating across the Concrete ecosystem, both on-chain and through community engagement.

There are two ways to participate:

* **Concrete Points:** Earned through on-chain participation in the Concrete protocol, including depositing assets into eligible Concrete Vaults.
* **Bags:** Earned by completing eligible social and community quests.

At a later point in the campaign, **Bags will be converted into Concrete Points**.

The social campaign is powered by [**Fuul**](https://fuul.xyz).

## Bags and Points

### Concrete Points

Concrete Points are earned through eligible on-chain activity across the Concrete protocol.

Users earn Concrete Points by depositing into eligible Concrete Vaults and participating in the protocol. This on-chain program runs alongside the social campaign.

### Bags

Bags represent rewards earned through Concrete's social campaign.

Users earn Bags by completing eligible quests and community actions through the campaign powered by Fuul.

Bags and Concrete Points are tracked separately during the campaign. At a later point, **Bags will be converted into Concrete Points**.

## Social Campaign

To begin earning Bags, connect your wallet and any required social accounts through the Concrete Points campaign powered by Fuul.

### 1. Connect Your Wallet and Accounts

Connect the wallet you want associated with your campaign activity and link any social accounts required to complete eligible quests.

Make sure your accounts remain connected so eligible activity can be properly tracked.

### 2. Complete Social Actions

Bags are earned through eligible **social and community actions**.

Examples may include, but are not limited to:

* **Following Concrete on X:** Stay updated on the latest developments from Concrete ([Concrete on X](https://x.com/ConcreteXYZ)).
* **Joining the Concrete Discord:** Join discussions, share feedback, and connect with the Concrete community ([Concrete Discord](https://discord.gg/concretexyz)).
* **Writing a Blog Article About Concrete:** Use the rubric provided in Discord to participate in the Article of the Week campaign.

Some quests may have prerequisites. If a task is shown as **Locked**, complete the required action first to unlock it.
